Study Overview

In this study, you will negotiate wholesale prices with an AI agent across multiple rounds.

Your Role

You will alternate between being a buyer (retailer) and a supplier.

How It Works

  • Each round, you'll negotiate a price with an AI agent
  • You can make offers, counteroffers, or accept the other party's offer
  • Rounds will have different configurations for reflection
  • Your goal is to reach a mutually acceptable deal

Negotiation Parameters

  • As a buyer: Price range $1-$99 (you want the lowest price)
  • As a supplier: Price range $31-$200 (you want the highest price)
  • Maximum turns per side: 5
  • Time limit per turn: 90 seconds
